## Defect (reproduced on 13d5c8a)
For `test` and `ci` claims, one passing proof was enough. Each of these was VERIFIED:
- a test pass at 10:00 plus a failing run (exit 1) at 11:00;
- a pass plus an unlinked failing run (exit 2);
- CI success plus another workflow on the same head with `failure`;
- CI success plus a `cancelled` run.

## Fix (+15 lines in pi/continuity-handoff.mjs)
- After the existing pass checks, any **fresh** `test_run` with a non-zero `exit_code`, or any `ci_run` that is
  `completed` with a conclusion other than `success`, demotes the claim with `conflicting_fresh_evidence(...)`.
  This applies whether or not the failing record has a link.
- Stale failures and still-running CI runs do not demote.
- The Trust limits section states the rule.

## Tightened expectation (reviewers: please confirm)
- Before this change, a test asserted that an `action_required` run beside a success counted as VERIFIED. It now
  counts as UNPROVEN, and the test expects UNPROVEN.
- A fail-then-pass history inside the freshness window now demotes the claim. Remove superseded records from the
  evidence file instead.

   heuristic on the claim text.
